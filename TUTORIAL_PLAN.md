# The tutorial, rebuilt for someone who has never traded an option

## The problem

The old tutorial opened the full trading screen on day one: lineup, chart with four studies, a payoff chart, a probability gauge, Greeks, a score preview, an analyst desk, four sliders, an order ticket, a cartridge rail, six top-bar gauges, plus a pop-up from the Director. Ines's tips sat in a box in the corner and talked about "a bull put", "POP" and "conviction" to someone who has never heard those words. An experienced trader can sort that out. A new trader can't, and quits.

## The idea in one line

**Start with an almost empty desk, light up one thing at a time, and say only what that thing is for.**

Three rules guide every lesson:

1. **One area at a time.** Everything except the part being taught is dimmed, and parts not taught yet are hidden. A spotlight (a lit cut-out with a pulsing outline) sits over the one thing Ines is talking about, with her speech bubble right next to it.
2. **Two sentences, plain words.** Each lesson says what the thing is for and what to do with it. No jargon until the screen shows it, and then it's explained in the same breath ("the credit: the money you're paid up front").
3. **Do, don't read.** Where it can, a lesson waits for the player to do the thing (pick a direction, move the line, press SELL, play a day) rather than asking them to read about it. Lessons that are only information have a GOT IT button (or Enter).

The order follows what the player asked for: **the goal of the game first, then how to make a trade, then the bonuses and powerups.**

## The lessons

The tutorial is a short practice year: Month 1, a shop, Month 2, a shop, and a Review. Each part of it teaches one layer.

### Part 1 · The goal (Month 1, before anything else)

The screen opens nearly empty: the top bar, and nothing else.

| Lesson | Spotlight on | What Ines says (short) | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Welcome | nothing (centered) | I'll show you one thing at a time. You don't need to know anything yet. | — |
| Your goal | the points bar and goal card | Each round has a points target. Winning trades earn points. Fill the bar to pass. | Goal bar, goal card |
| Your safety line | the Max-Loss gauge | This is how much you can lose before the round ends. Win points, stay above the line. | Max-Loss gauge |

### Part 2 · Your first trade (Month 1)

| Lesson | Spotlight on | What Ines says | Waits for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pick a stock | the lineup | Each card is a real stock at a real moment in history, name hidden. | GOT IT |
| Read the chart | the chart (candles and volume only) | Each bar is a day. Green closed higher, red lower. The right edge is today. | GOT IT |
| Up or down? | the chart, with two big buttons in the bubble | Where do you think it goes in the next few weeks? Close is good enough. | the player picks ▲ UP or ▼ DOWN |
| Your line | the strike line on the chart | You're paid now, and you keep it while the stock stays above (or below) this line until the trade ends. | GOT IT |
| Paid up front | the SELL button | SELL pays this credit right now. "risk" is the most you can lose. POP is the chance you keep money. | GOT IT |
| Safer or richer | the strike slider | Drag it (or the handle on the chart). Farther from the price is safer but pays less. | the player moves the line (or KEEP IT) |
| Place it | the SELL button | Happy with it? Press SELL. | a trade is placed |
| You're in | the lineup card | The card says IN TRADE with your profit or loss; the box on the chart is the trade. | GOT IT |

Until the trade is placed the clock is locked, so Space can't skip past the lesson by accident.

### Part 3 · Letting time pass (Month 1)

| Lesson | Spotlight on | What Ines says | Waits for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Play a day | the clock buttons | Press Space (or the button) to play one day. | a day is played |
| The recap *(when it first appears)* | the day's recap | Every day you see how each trade stands. Each quiet day pays you a little: time decay. | GOT IT |
| Your exit plan | the positions panel | Every trade has a plan: take the win at half the max profit, cut a loss at 2× the credit. The clock stops when either is hit. | GOT IT |
| Keep going | no spotlight, a small corner bubble | Keep pressing Space, or pick 2X to let days roll. I'll speak up when something happens. | the round ends |

Moments that can happen any time get a lesson the first time they happen: **a decision point** (close at your plan: it scores a bonus and keeps stress down; holding a loser past the stop costs you, aimed straight at the "holding losers" habit), **a trade closing**, **stress going up**, and **END ROUND** becoming available.

### Part 4 · The score (the tally)

| Lesson | Spotlight on | What Ines says |
|---|---|---|
| The tally | the receipts | Each closed trade prints a receipt: chips × mult = points. That's how good trading becomes score. |
| Continue | none | When the count finishes, press CONTINUE. |

### Part 5 · Bonuses and powerups (the first shop)

The first shop shows only four windows; analysts, memos, the playbook and vouchers stay hidden.

| Lesson | Spotlight on | What Ines says |
|---|---|---|
| Shop cash | your cash and LAST ROUND | Passing a round pays shop cash. Spend it here on upgrades for this run. |
| Cartridges | CARTRIDGES | Cartridges are powerups: each changes how your trades score. Hover one to read it. |
| Your desk | YOUR DESK | What you own. Cartridges fire left to right, so order matters. |
| Families | YOUR BUILD | Every cartridge has a family. Own 2, 3 or 4 of one family for extra bonuses. |
| Buy one | none | Buy one you can afford (or save), then press NEXT ROUND. |

### Part 6 · More tools (Month 2)

| Lesson | Spotlight on | What Ines says | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tickets | TICKETS | Tickets are how many trades you can open this round. Try two different stocks. | tickets, cash, equity |
| How long | the Expires slider | How long the trade lasts. Short trades move fast; longer ones pay more but take longer. | Expires |
| How big | the Size and Width sliders | Size is how many contracts (more points, more risk). Width is the gap to your safety leg, which caps the loss. | Size, Width |
| The brief | the BRIEF panel | News, earnings dates and how much the stock usually moves. Earnings can make a stock jump. | the right panel |
| The payoff | the TRADE panel | What you make or lose at each price when the trade ends. | — |
| Reroll / sit out | the round controls | Don't like the cards? REROLL deals new ones. SIT OUT skips the round for a small reward. | round controls |
| Your turn | none | Two trades on two stocks, then play the round out. | the round ends |

### Part 7 · More upgrades (the second shop)

Analysts (more information, not luck), Memos (one-use tricks), the Playbook (level up a trade type) and Vouchers (permanent upgrades) each get one lesson as their window appears.

### Part 8 · The Review and the whole desk

A one-line explanation of the Review (a boss round with a special rule), then in the Review round: the cartridge rail ("your powerups, firing as trades score"), the IF IT WINS box ("what a trade is worth before you place it"), and finally **everything else switches on** (structure cards, presets, order types, studies) with "That's the whole desk."

## Always available

- **SKIP LESSONS** in every bubble: turns the whole desk on and stops the lessons (the run carries on).
- The lessons remember where you are: leave and resume the tutorial and Ines picks up at the same lesson. Starting a new tutorial starts from the top.
- Director Kessler's pop-up lines are muted in the tutorial so only one voice talks at a time.
- Every lesson change has a sound and a short glow on whatever just appeared.

## What it does not change

The market is the same real data, the trades are real trades on it, and scoring is unchanged. The tutorial only decides what is shown and when, and locks the clock until the first trade is placed.
