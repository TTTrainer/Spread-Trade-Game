# Bosses: art brief

The 12 bosses currently show their **market logo** (the existing Review pictures) on the case file and the chart banner. When their portraits and pillar icons arrive, they take over: the portrait on the case file, the pillar icon beside the pillar name.

## Style

Same as the rest of the game: pixel art, crisp pixels, 1-pixel dark outline, 3–4 shade steps, deep indigo shadows, each boss lit in its own color (listed above), no text or logos. All characters are fictional.
